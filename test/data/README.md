# Test data provenance

## AF-M3YHX5-F1-model_v4_hydrogens.cif / AF-M3YHX5-F1-model_v4_hydrogens_CYX.cif

AlphaFold DB structure prediction for UniProt entry
[M3YHX5](https://www.uniprot.org/uniprotkb/M3YHX5), chosen because its
sequence contains all 20 standard amino acids. Distributed under
[CC-BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Hydrogens were
added with [UCSF ChimeraX](https://www.cgl.ucsf.edu/chimerax/).

The `_CYX` variant has residues 37 and 69 renamed from `CYS` to `CYX` (the
Amber name for a disulfide-bonded cysteine) with their thiol hydrogens
removed, so that `bondparametrization` exercises the CYX residue templates.

Please cite:

- Jumper, J. et al. "Highly accurate protein structure prediction with
  AlphaFold." *Nature* 596, 583–589 (2021).
  https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-021-03819-2
- Varadi, M. et al. "AlphaFold Protein Structure Database: massively
  expanding the structural coverage of protein-sequence space with
  high-accuracy models." *Nucleic Acids Research* 50, D439–D444 (2022).
  https://doi.org/10.1093/nar/gkab1061

## 2qmt_H.cif

Derived from [PDB entry 2QMT](https://www.rcsb.org/structure/2QMT), an
experimentally determined, well-folded structure of the protein G B1
domain (Frericks Schmidt et al. 2007). PDB coordinate data is not subject
to copyright. Hydrogens were added with
[UCSF ChimeraX](https://www.cgl.ucsf.edu/chimerax/).

Please cite:

- Frericks Schmidt, H.L., Sperling, L.J., Gao, Y.G., Wylie, B.J.,
  Boettcher, J.M., Wilson, S.R., Rienstra, C.M. "Crystal polymorphism of
  protein GB1 examined by solid-state NMR spectroscopy and X-ray
  diffraction." *J. Phys. Chem. B* 111, 14362–14369 (2007).
  https://doi.org/10.1021/jp075531p
